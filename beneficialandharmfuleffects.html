Beneficial and Harmful Effects of Computing

Decide if each scenario is a Beneficial or Harmful effect of computing. Click your answer to get instant feedback!

Beneficial Effects

  • Improves communication (instant messaging, social media)
  • Increases access to information (search engines, online learning)
  • Enhances efficiency and productivity (automation, AI tools)
  • Advances healthcare (medical data, diagnostics, telemedicine)
  • Enables innovation and creativity (apps, games, digital content)

Harmful Effects

  • Privacy risks (data tracking, breaches, surveillance)
  • Cybersecurity threats (hacking, malware, identity theft)
  • Digital divide (unequal access to technology)
  • Addiction and mental health issues (excessive screen time, social media pressure)
  • Misinformation (fake news, biased algorithms)

Key Idea

Most technologies have both positive and negative impacts, so you should consider who is affected and how.
